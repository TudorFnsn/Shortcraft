## 3. Findings and proposals (ranked)

1. **Premium video is where all the cost is (~50x a standard video).** Measured $0.064 per video-second vs the
   catalog's conservative $0.12. The catalog is ~1.9x pessimistic, so either (a) keep 1,420 credits/s and bank the
   extra margin, or (b) cut the credit price toward ~800/s to make Premium more attractive and still hold > 3x.
   Needs 20+ measured premium jobs first. *Decision: owner.*
2. **Make premium opt-in per scene** (AI video only for the hook scene/first 3-5s, Ken-Burns for the rest): cuts premium
   cost ~50-70% with little perceived loss. Code change, no price change. *Implementable by profit-implementer.*
3. **Re-price the video slot** (fal Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro = $0.07/s list, bills fixed 5s clips). Leads, unverified, must be
   checked on the vendors' own pricing pages before any swap: Seedance 2.x Fast reported $0.022-0.15/s (sources conflict),
   Veo 3.1 Lite ~$0.05/s, Wan 2.6 ~$0.35 per 5s clip (~$0.07/s), Kling 3.0 Pro from $0.112/s, MuAPI/Atlas Cloud/other
   resellers claim 20-50% under fal. Realistic saving: 0-60% of the premium line. Run a side-by-side quality test
   (`scripts/live-providers-smoke.mts`) on 2-3 candidates; adapter swap = one adapter + one `MODELS` row.
4. **Standard tier is already near-free** ($0.04/video): the lever there is conversion and credits breakage, not cost.
   Script writer is ~$0.04 of that: try a Haiku-class model + prompt caching for -70% of that line.
5. **Annual plans**: price ids exist (`stripePriceIdYearly`); 2 months free = ~17% discount for cash up front and lower churn.
6. **Trial burn**: 3,000 trial credits ≈ 1.5 standard videos ≈ $0.06 per signup: cheap, keep (do not allow premium on trial).
7. **Infra**: Vercel **Hobby is non-commercial**; budget Vercel Pro (~$20/mo) before taking live payments. Supabase project
   `AIAssist` is INACTIVE and unrelated: delete if unused. Storage/egress per MP4 not measured yet (Q7).
